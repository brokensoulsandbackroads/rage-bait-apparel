Privacy Policy

This explains what personal information Rage Bait Apparel collects, why we use it, who we may share it with and the choices you have.

Who we are

Rage Bait Apparel is a trading brand of Broken Souls & Backroads, operated by Tony Stanton as a sole trader.

Tony Stanton, trading as Broken Souls & Backroads / Rage Bait Apparel, is responsible for the personal information covered by this policy.

Business address: 7 Marlborough Gardens, Faringdon, Oxfordshire, SN7 7DE, United Kingdom.

For privacy questions, requests or concerns, email crew@ragebaitapparel.co.uk.

What we collect

Join the Crew: your email address and the date/time you signed up.

Orders: when you place an order, we may collect your name, email address, telephone number, delivery address, order details, transaction references and payment status. PayPal processes the payment itself, so Rage Bait Apparel does not need to receive or store your full card details.

Messages: information you provide when you contact us about an order, return, delivery or other enquiry.

Technical information: our website and infrastructure providers may process information such as IP address, device/browser information and request logs where needed to deliver, secure and protect the site.

Why we use your information

We use personal information to run the mailing list, process and fulfil orders, provide customer support, deal with returns or delivery issues, prevent abuse and fraud, maintain the security of our services, and meet legal or accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Our lawful bases

Consent: for Join the Crew marketing emails. You can withdraw that consent at any time.

Contract: where we need your information to take steps at your request before a purchase, process an order, deliver it or provide related customer service.

Legal obligation: where we must retain or use information to meet tax, accounting, consumer-law or other legal requirements.

Legitimate interests: where reasonably necessary to operate and secure the website, prevent fraud or misuse, manage the business and handle disputes, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights.

Marketing emails

When you join the Crew, you are asking Rage Bait Apparel to send you marketing emails about new drops, products, offers and brand news.

You can unsubscribe at any time. Every marketing email we send will include a simple way to opt out, or you can email crew@ragebaitapparel.co.uk.

After you unsubscribe, we may keep the minimum information needed on a suppression list so we can respect your request and avoid marketing to you again by mistake.

Who we share information with

We only share personal information where needed to operate the business or provide the service you requested.

PayPal processes checkout payments and receives the information needed to complete and protect the transaction. Two Fifteen receives the product, delivery and contact information needed to print and fulfil made-to-order products. Delivery information may then be provided to the relevant postal or courier service so the parcel can be delivered and tracked.

Our infrastructure also uses services including Cloudflare and GitHub Pages. We may use other service providers where reasonably needed for website hosting, security, email, accounting, customer service or professional advice.

We may also disclose information where required by law, regulation, court order or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

International data transfers

Some of the service providers we use may process or store information outside the UK. Where personal information is transferred internationally, we expect appropriate legal safeguards to be used where required by UK data protection law.

How long we keep information

Mailing-list information is kept while you remain subscribed, unless we no longer need it. If you unsubscribe, we may retain limited suppression information so your opt-out can continue to be honoured.

Order, payment-reference, accounting and customer-service records are kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose they were collected, including any period required by tax, accounting, consumer or legal obligations and for handling possible disputes or claims.

We review retention and remove or anonymise information when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to ask for access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, or to object to certain uses of your information.

You have the right to object to direct marketing at any time.

Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise a right, email crew@ragebaitapparel.co.uk. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Cookies and similar technology

We do not currently use advertising or analytics cookies ourselves. Our hosting, payment or security providers may use technology that is strictly necessary to deliver, secure or protect their services.

If we introduce non-essential cookies or tracking in future, we will update our information and ask for consent where UK law requires it.

Automated decisions

We do not currently use your personal information to make solely automated decisions about you or to carry out profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

Complaints

If you have a concern, please contact us first at crew@ragebaitapparel.co.uk so we can try to resolve it.

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office. Visit ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, providers or legal obligations change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
