Shipping & Delivery

The delivery estimates below are Royal Mail transit times after dispatch. Production time is additional and may vary by product.

International orders

International orders can occasionally take longer to arrive while passing through customs.

Customers are responsible for any customs duties, import taxes, handling fees or other local charges applied by the destination country. These charges are not included in our shipping price.

Something gone wrong?

If your tracking has stopped updating, your parcel appears lost, or something arrives damaged, email crew@ragebaitapparel.co.uk with your order number and we will help sort it.
