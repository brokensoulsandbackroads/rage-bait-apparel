Outlaw Tee Size Guide

The Outlaw Tee uses the Bella+Canvas CV001 unisex jersey T-shirt. Grab a tee you already like, lay it flat and compare the measurements below.

How to measure

1. Half chest Lay the T-shirt flat and measure straight across from armpit to armpit. Do not measure all the way around your chest.
2. Body length Measure from the highest point of the shoulder down to the bottom hem.

Fit

This is a unisex classic-fit T-shirt. Because everybody wears a tee differently, comparing these garment measurements with a shirt you already own is the safest way to choose your size.

Measurements are supplied by the garment manufacturer and small production tolerances can occur.
